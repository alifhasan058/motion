Branding & Digital

Brew Crew

Branding for a generation on the go.

Brew Crew is a modern coffee brand built for creatives, dreamers, and go-getters. We created a bold, energetic brand identity and digital assets that capture their mission: great coffee, real people, anywhere.

Year
2024
Services
Branding, Web Design, Illustration, Motion
Client
Brew Crew

Project overview

More than coffee. It's a movement.

Brew Crew exists to fuel the creative spirit. We developed a vibrant brand identity, custom illustration style, and a responsive website that feels fresh, friendly, and full of energy — just like the people it's made for.

From the logo to the packaging, every touchpoint was designed to feel approachable, authentic, and ready for life on the go.
